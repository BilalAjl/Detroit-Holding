The Three Strategic Pillars of Detroit Holding

Origination-Driven Value Creation

Detroit Holding positions itself upstream in the value chain by mastering the origination of opportunities across its core sectors. By identifying projects at an early stage—land, concessions, licenses or operating platforms—the group secures attractive entry points, structures investment-ready projects and captures a significant share of value creation before institutional capital is deployed.

Sector-Focused Platforms with Centralized Capital Discipline

Each activity is carried through a dedicated sector platform—real estate, agriculture, energy, investment management and professional services—while operating under a centralized capital allocation and governance framework. This structure combines deep sector expertise with disciplined investment decision-making, enabling synergies across platforms without strategic dilution.

Partnership-Led Scaling and Capital Recycling

Detroit Holding’s growth model is built on structured partnerships with institutional investors, industrial operators and international groups. Through co-development, partial exits and capital recycling, the group continuously redeploys capital into new opportunities, creating a virtuous cycle of scalable growth and sustainable, long-term value creation.

Buying property – what you need to know

1.
What is the process of buying a property internationally?
Buying property internationally involves researching markets, understanding local laws, securing financing, consulting experts, performing due diligence, and completing all legal and transactional steps carefully
2.
How long does the purchasing process take?
The purchasing process duration varies depending on the property, location, legal requirements, and financing, typically ranging from a few weeks to several months for international transactions
3.
Is it safe to invest in overseas real estate?
Investing in overseas real estate can be safe when you research markets, understand local laws, work with trusted professionals, and perform thorough due diligence before committing to any property
4.
Is property management available?
Yes, professional property management services are available, handling maintenance, tenant relations, rent collection, and legal compliance, ensuring your investment is well-managed efficiently and stress-free
